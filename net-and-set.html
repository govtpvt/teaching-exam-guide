UGC NET and State Eligibility Tests for Assistant Professors

To be considered for assistant professor posts in Indian colleges and universities you generally need a postgraduate degree and a qualifying result in the National Eligibility Test or a State Eligibility Test, unless you are exempt on the basis of a PhD under current regulations.

UGC NET

A national computer based test held in a large number of subjects. Paper 1 tests teaching and research aptitude; Paper 2 tests your subject. The result places candidates in categories that decide whether they qualify for a fellowship, for assistant professor eligibility, or for PhD admission only.

State Eligibility Test

Conducted by a state agency or university with UGC accreditation, in the same two-paper format. It qualifies you for assistant professor posts in universities and colleges of that state only. It does not carry a fellowship.

After the exam

Both publish a provisional answer key and invite challenges before the final key. For a worked example, see the Rajasthan SET 2026 answer key page. The qualifying certificate is issued online and does not expire for assistant professor eligibility.

Eligibility is not appointment

Colleges and public service commissions advertise posts separately, and selection involves academic scores, a written test in some states and an interview. Vacancies appear in the latest government jobs list; results and keys on govtpvt.com.
