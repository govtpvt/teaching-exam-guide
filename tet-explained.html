CTET and State TET Explained: Papers, Validity and Use

The Teacher Eligibility Test exists because the law requires a minimum standard for anyone appointed to teach classes 1 to 8. CBSE conducts the central test, CTET; each state conducts its own.

Two papers

  • Paper I for those who want to teach classes 1 to 5.
  • Paper II for classes 6 to 8, with a choice between mathematics and science or social studies.

You may sit both. Each paper is objective, has no negative marking, and covers child development and pedagogy, two languages and the subject areas for that level.

Qualifying marks

The pass mark is a fixed percentage, with a lower mark permitted for reserved categories under the rules of the appointing government. There is no merit list at this stage: you either qualify or you do not.

Validity

The qualifying certificate is now valid for life, and there is no limit on the number of attempts. You can re-appear to improve your score, which matters because many recruitments give weight to TET marks.

CTET or state TET?

CertificateAccepted for
CTETCentral government schools such as KVS and NVS, schools in union territories, and many states and private schools that choose to accept it
State TETSchools of that state government; some states accept only their own TET for state posts

What it does not do

A TET certificate is not an appointment and not a rank. You still have to apply when vacancies are advertised and clear the recruitment exam. Watch for those notifications in the latest jobs list, and keep your certificate and marks statement safe for document verification.
